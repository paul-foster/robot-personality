# Team Profile

* Team name: MECO Robotics
* Rookie season: 2020

# About MECO Robotics

A small community team in Bithlo, Florida, we meet at Orange County Academy, a small private school serving the under privileged. 

We have students from all around east Orlando. 

Our mentors are a diverse group of eager college freshman at the University of Central Florida (UCF) to passionate adults from a wide variety of vocations.

# Our Mission Statement

MECO Robotics empowers underserved students in East Orlando by providing a safe, equitable, and engaging environment where they can explore robotics while creating lasting relationships. 

We are dedicated to nurturing curiosity, resilience, confidence, and collaboration, enabling students to experience growth through both their successes and challenges. 

We launch future leaders who excel personally and make a positive impact in their communities.
