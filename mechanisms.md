# Robot Mechanisms

## Intake

The intake uses belt driven rollers to injest balls into the hopper.

## Hopper

The hopper can extend after the match starts to both agitate balls toward the shooter and expand capacity during inteke. Two racks driven by geared motors extend or retract. When fully extended, a flip down bar reaches over the bumpers to guide balls in.

## Shooter

A wide shooter can shoot up to 3 balls simulaneously, fed by a series of rollers. The shooter has a hood that can adjust the angle by up to 15 degrees allowing for up to the goal shots and as far away as the climber.
